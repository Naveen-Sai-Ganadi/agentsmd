## Takeaway

Sync is fully commodified. **The lint/CI-gate lane is now actively contested**:
`agnix` (LSP + lint), `seojoonkim/agentlinter` (ESLint framing, 30 rules), and
`agent-audit` (security angle) all landed in the same 7-day window as agentsmd
v0.1.0. Our defensible wedge is narrowing to:

- (a) **enterprise/monorepo policy-as-code** (nested nearest-wins, per-team
  overrides, org-wide grade floor);
- (b) **being the auditable rubric GitHub's "2,500 repos" post implicitly asks
  for** — 1:1 map our `audit` dimensions to the GitHub Blog criteria and cite
  them in the README.

Pick one and move fast before agnix's LSP story or agentlinter's rule-count
story defines the category. Feeds decision #12 (v0.1.1 focus: monorepo vs. drift).
Given (a) above, **monorepo mode looks like the higher-EV bet now**.
